Our Story

Rooted in Dubai. Built for global cross-border trade.

New Nexus General Trading L.L.C. is a comprehensive international cross-border trade and industrial operation company headquartered in Dubai, United Arab Emirates — focused on global bulk commodity trade and high-tech hardware equipment trading.

Founding Background

Founded to close the gap in cross-border trade

The company was founded based on the booming cross-border trade demand between China, the Middle East and Central Asia. Rooted in Dubai's superior international trade and financial policy environment, our core founding team brings decades of experience in cross-border bulk cargo trading and international business negotiation.

We aim to build a standardized, transparent and efficient global cross-border trade service platform — solving the pain points of information asymmetry, inefficient transaction and non-compliant cross-border operation in international trade.
